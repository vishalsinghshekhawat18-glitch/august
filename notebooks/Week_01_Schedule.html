Goal of this week 1) Get an honest baseline: a cold, timed Prelims paper on Monday. 2) Set up the system: error log, hours log, revision tracker, current-affairs file. 3) Finish chapters 1–6 of the Rajasthan History notebook at full depth, with answer writing from day one. 4) Every block, every day. Missed blocks are reported, not hidden.

1. The rules

  1. Six blocks, every day. The clock times below assume a free day. If something else fixes part of your day, move the blocks around it, but do not drop or shorten one. The blocks are the plan; the clock is only a suggestion.
  2. Deep study is phone-free. Phone in another room, notifications off.
  3. Write before you read the model. Every Mains answer is handwritten, timed and inside the word limit before you open the model answer.
  4. Every mistake is logged, with its reason: did not know · confused two facts · misread the question · careless · ran out of time.
  5. Sleep 7 hours. Memory is consolidated in sleep, so it is part of revision, not time taken from it.
  6. Log hours honestly, every day, block by block. A short real number beats a long imagined one.
