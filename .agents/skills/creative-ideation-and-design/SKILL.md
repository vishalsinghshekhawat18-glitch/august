# Creative Ideation & Design Thinking

This skill generates original concepts, breaks creative deadlocks, and systematically guides innovation using human-centered Design Thinking and lateral ideation frameworks.

## 1. The 5-Stage Design Thinking Workflow

```text
Empathize  ──►  Define  ──►  Ideate  ──►  Prototype  ──►  Test
```

1. **Empathize (Deep User Understanding)**:
   - Identify unarticulated frustrations, emotional desires, and daily friction points.
   - Separate stated needs ("I want a faster horse") from underlying desires ("I want to get there faster").
2. **Define (Reframing the Problem)**:
   - Formulate a clear "How Might We" (HMW) statement:
   - *Formula*: *"How might we [action] for [target user] so that [desired outcome without specifying the solution]?"*
3. **Ideate (Divergent Brainstorming)**:
   - Prioritize sheer quantity over quality. Suspend judgment and critique.
   - Encourage wild, extreme, and unconventional suggestions.
4. **Prototype (Low-Fidelity Tangibility)**:
   - Translate abstract ideas into rapid sketches, outlines, wireframes, or 1-page concepts that can be evaluated in minutes.
5. **Test & Learn**:
   - Put prototypes in front of real users, observe points of friction or delight, and iterate.

## 2. The SCAMPER Ideation Catalyst

When stuck in conventional thinking, run the problem through each SCAMPER lever:

- **S (Substitute)**: What materials, steps, people, or assumptions can be swapped out?
- **C (Combine)**: Can we merge two unrelated ideas, products, or industries together?
- **A (Adapt)**: What other industry solved a similar problem? How can we copy their model?
- **M (Modify / Magnify / Minify)**: What if we make it 10x larger, 10x smaller, faster, louder, or completely silent?
- **P (Put to Other Use)**: Who else could benefit from this solution in an entirely different context?
- **E (Eliminate)**: What happens if we remove the most expensive, complex, or core component?
- **R (Reverse / Rearrange)**: What if the sequence was reversed? What if the customer paid us *after* or was paid by us?

## 3. Lateral Thinking & Oblique Prompts

- **Inversion (Anti-Problem)**: *"How could we design the worst possible experience for this user?"* List all answers, then invert them into creative solutions.
- **Forced Analogies**: Pick a random noun (e.g., *a lighthouse, a beehive, an anchor, an espresso machine*). Ask: *"In what 3 ways is our project exactly like this object?"*
- **Extreme Constraints**:
  - *"If this had to launch by 5:00 PM today, what would we ship?"*
  - *"If we could not use screens or text, how would this work?"*

## 4. Concept Selection Matrix (2x2)

Map brainstormed ideas across two axes:
- **Feasibility (Ease of Execution)** vs. **Originality / Impact (Breakthrough Potential)**
- Prioritize **"Game Changers"** (High Impact + Moderate/High Feasibility) and **"Quick Wins"** (Moderate Impact + High Feasibility).
