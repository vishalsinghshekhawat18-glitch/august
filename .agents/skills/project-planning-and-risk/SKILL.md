# Project Planning & Risk Assessment

This skill structures complex projects into actionable, phased roadmaps with clear accountability and proactive risk mitigation plans.

## Procedure

### 1. Project Charter & Objectives
- **Objective Statement**: Clear, measurable definition of project success (KPIs / OKRs).
- **Scope & Non-Scope**: Explicitly distinguish what is in scope vs. what is out of scope to prevent scope creep.
- **Key Stakeholders**: Primary sponsor, project lead, and core contributors.

### 2. Phased Roadmap & Milestones
Break down the initiative into logical, sequential phases:

```text
Phase 1: Discovery & Scoping  ──►  Phase 2: Execution & Drafting  ──►  Phase 3: Review & Refinement  ──►  Phase 4: Launch & Handover
```

For each phase, document:
- **Phase Objective**: What must be achieved.
- **Key Deliverables**: Tangible outputs.
- **Milestone Gate**: Criteria required before proceeding to the next phase.

### 3. RACI Responsibility Matrix
Ensure absolute clarity on roles for each major deliverable:

| Deliverable / Milestone | Responsible (R) | Accountable (A) | Consulted (C) | Informed (I) |
|-------------------------|-----------------|-----------------|---------------|--------------|
| Project Charter         | [Doer]          | [Final Signoff] | [Expert]      | [Audience]   |
| Implementation Plan     | [Doer]          | [Final Signoff] | [Expert]      | [Audience]   |

*Legend:*
- **R (Responsible)**: The person(s) who complete the task.
- **A (Accountable)**: The single individual with ultimate veto/signoff power.
- **C (Consulted)**: Subject matter experts providing input.
- **I (Informed)**: People kept updated on progress.

### 4. Pre-Mortem & Risk Register
Assume the project has failed 6 months in the future. Identify the failure modes and build defenses:

| Risk Description | Category (Tech/People/Timeline) | Likelihood (1-5) | Impact (1-5) | Severity Score | Preventive Mitigation Plan | Contingency Trigger |
|------------------|---------------------------------|------------------|--------------|----------------|----------------------------|---------------------|
| [Potential risk] | Timeline                        | 3                | 4            | 12             | [Proactive defense]        | [What to do if hits]|

### 5. Review Cadence & Communication Plan
- **Status Updates**: Frequency (e.g., weekly async memo, bi-weekly standup).
- **Escalation Path**: Procedure when a milestone slips by more than 3 days.
