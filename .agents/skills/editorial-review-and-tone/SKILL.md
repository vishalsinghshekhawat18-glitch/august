# Editorial Review & Tone Polishing

This skill provides a rigorous multi-pass editorial review to turn drafts into punchy, persuasive, and impeccably polished communications.

## 4-Pass Editorial Methodology

### Pass 1: Architecture & Structural Flow
- **Macro Logic**: Does the document flow naturally from introduction to conclusion?
- **Paragraph Integrity**: Does each paragraph express exactly one central thought?
- **Transitions**: Are bridges between sections smooth or jarring?

### Pass 2: Clarity & Word Economy (The "30% Trim")
- **Eliminate Fluff**: Cut empty introductory phrases (e.g., "It is important to note that", "At this point in time").
- **Passive to Active Voice**: Convert passive constructions ("The report was submitted by the team") to active ones ("The team submitted the report").
- **Direct Vocabulary**: Replace weak verb-noun phrases with punchy verbs (e.g., "reach an agreement" ➔ "agree", "conduct an investigation into" ➔ "investigate").

### Pass 3: Tone & Voice Alignment
Adjust based on the user's intended register:
- **Executive / Leadership**: Confident, data-backed, decisive, concise.
- **Client / Customer Facing**: Empathetic, solutions-focused, accessible, respectful.
- **Persuasive / Pitch**: Compelling hooks, clear value props, frictionless calls to action.
- **Internal / Collaborative**: Clear, collegial, direct, actionable.

### Pass 4: Mechanics, Grammar & Scannability
- Check spelling, punctuation, capitalization, and syntax.
- Verify formatting consistency (bullet points, parallel grammatical structure in lists).
- Ensure key takeaways are visually scannable using bold anchor words.

## Output Format

When returning editorial feedback:
1. **Editorial Summary**: Brief assessment of strengths and the primary areas improved.
2. **Key Changes & Rationale**: Highlight 2–3 major structural or tonal shifts made.
3. **Polished Version**: The complete, ready-to-use polished text.
4. *(Optional)* **Before & After Comparison**: For critical sections to show improvements clearly.
