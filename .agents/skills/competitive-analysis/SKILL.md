# Competitive Analysis & Market Positioning

This skill provides a structured methodology to analyze competitors, benchmark market positioning, and identify defensible white spaces.

## Analysis Dimensions

### 1. Market Landscape Overview
- **Direct Competitors**: Offerings solving the exact same problem for the same audience.
- **Indirect Competitors**: Different solutions addressing the same underlying need.
- **Substitutes & Workarounds**: Spreadsheets, manual work, internal teams doing it themselves.

### 2. Deep-Dive Competitor Profiling
For each key player evaluate:
- **Value Proposition**: The promise they make to customers in one sentence.
- **Target Audience / ICP**: Enterprise, Mid-Market, SMB, or Prosumer.
- **Pricing & Packaging Strategy**: Free tier, seat-based, usage-based, or enterprise custom quotes.
- **Key Strengths**: Core competencies and reasons customers choose them.
- **Vulnerabilities & Customer Complaints**: Missing capabilities, poor support, complexity, high costs.
- **Moat / Defensibility**: Network effects, proprietary data, switching costs, or brand equity.

### 4. Strategic Positioning & Whitespace Identification
- **Perceptual Map Dimensions**: Choose two axes of competition (e.g., Low Cost vs. Premium, Niche vs. All-in-One).
- **Identified Whitespaces**: Underserved customer segments or unmet user needs.
- **Counter-Positioning Strategy**: How to position against the leader so their strengths become their weaknesses.
- **Win Themes**: 3 clear arguments sales or marketing should emphasize to win deals against them.
