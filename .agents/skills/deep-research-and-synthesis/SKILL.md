# Deep Research & Knowledge Synthesis

This skill analyzes extensive sources, long-form documents, and complex subject matter to produce balanced, high-signal executive intelligence briefs.

## Synthesis Methodology

### 1. Source Evaluation & Triangulation
- **Primary vs. Secondary**: Distinguish primary empirical evidence from commentary or derivative reporting.
- **Fact vs. Opinion**: Explicitly separate verified metrics from speculative assertions.
- **Contrasting Viewpoints**: Identify where industry experts or data sources disagree and why.

### 2. High-Signal Extraction
Extract and tag:
- **Core Thesis**: The central finding or argument.
- **Key Statistics & Metrics**: Quantitative evidence with source attribution.
- **Quotable Insights**: Memorable formulations or definitions.
- **Underlying Drivers**: What systemic shifts (regulatory, economic, behavioral) are driving this trend?

### 3. Output Structure: Executive Intelligence Brief

```markdown
# [Topic Title]: Executive Briefing

## Executive Takeaway
[2–3 sentence synthesis of the most critical finding and its practical consequence.]

## Core Findings & Insights
- **[Key Theme 1]**: Explanation backed by specific data or evidence.
- **[Key Theme 2]**: Explanation backed by specific data or evidence.
- **[Key Theme 3]**: Explanation backed by specific data or evidence.

## Data & Evidence Summary
| Metric / Indicator | Observed Value | Context / Trend | Source |
|--------------------|----------------|-----------------|--------|
| [e.g., Market Size]| [$X billion]   | [+Y% CAGR]      | [Org]  |

## Critical Nuances & Counter-Arguments
- [Caveat or dissenting view held by reputable analysts]
- [Risks or blind spots in current data]

## Strategic Implications & Recommended Actions
1. **Immediate**: Actionable step based on these findings.
2. **Medium-Term**: Strategic adjustment or monitoring priority.
```

## Quality Guardrails
- **No Filler**: Zero generic transitional statements.
- **Explicit Assumptions**: Highlight when data is based on projections or estimates.
- **Actionable Context**: Connect every research finding back to "what this means for decisions".
