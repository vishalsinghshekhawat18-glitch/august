# Executive Memo & Business Proposal

This skill writes concise, persuasive, high-impact business memos structured specifically for C-suite and executive readers with limited time.

## Guiding Principles
- **BLUF (Bottom Line Up Front)**: Never bury the lead. State the recommendation, decision, and impact in the first 30 seconds of reading.
- **Data-Driven & Quantified**: Replace vague adjectives ("significant increase", "faster") with concrete numbers, percentages, or timeline projections.
- **Structured Scannability**: Use bold lead-ins, tables, and short paragraphs. No walls of text.

## Standard Structure

### 1. Header Block
```text
TO:       [Stakeholder / Leadership Group]
FROM:     [Author / Team]
DATE:     [Date]
SUBJECT:  [Concise, decision-oriented subject line]
ACTION:   [e.g., For Decision / For Approval / For Information]
```

### 2. Executive Summary (BLUF)
A self-contained 2–4 sentence summary answering:
1. What is being proposed or decided?
2. Why is it needed now (urgency / opportunity)?
3. What is the expected upside, cost, and primary risk?

### 3. Problem Statement & Strategic Context
- The current bottleneck, market shift, or inefficiency.
- The business cost of inaction (what happens if we do nothing?).
- Alignment with broader organizational goals.

### 4. Evaluated Options
Present evaluated pathways objectively:

| Pathway | Description | Pros | Cons / Trade-offs | Est. Cost & Timeline |
|---------|-------------|------|-------------------|----------------------|
| **Option A (Recommended)** | [Approach description] | [High impact] | [Resource need] | [$X / Y weeks] |
| **Option B (Alternative)**  | [Alternative approach] | [Lower cost] | [Slower impact] | [$X / Y weeks] |
| **Option C (Status Quo)**   | Do nothing | No spend | Ongoing degradation | Opportunity loss |

### 5. Detailed Recommendation & Business Impact
Deep-dive into the recommended pathway:
- Revenue, cost-saving, efficiency, or strategic benefits.
- Resource allocation required (people, budget, tools).
- Key performance metrics (KPIs) to measure success.

### 6. Risk Mitigation & Next Steps
- Top 2–3 anticipated risks and preventative controls.
- Immediate actions needed upon approval within the first 14 days.
- **Specific Signoff / Approval Request**: Clearly state what decision is required.
