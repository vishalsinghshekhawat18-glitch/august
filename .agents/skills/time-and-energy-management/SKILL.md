# Time & Energy Management Systems

This skill crafts sustainable, high-leverage personal operating systems that maximize deep work, eliminate cognitive overwhelm, and align high-friction priorities with natural energy rhythms.

## 1. Prioritization & Triage Frameworks

### The Eisenhower Matrix (Action Orientation)
- **Quadrant 1 (Urgent & Important)**: Crises, pressing deadlines. ➔ *Do immediately with focus.*
- **Quadrant 2 (Not Urgent but Important)**: Strategy, health, relationships, deep skill building. ➔ *Schedule dedicated calendar blocks (This is where high performance lives).*
- **Quadrant 3 (Urgent but Not Important)**: Interruptions, non-critical meetings, sudden pings. ➔ *Delegate, batch, or automate.*
- **Quadrant 4 (Not Urgent & Not Important)**: Mindless browsing, busywork. ➔ *Eliminate ruthlessly.*

### The "Rule of 3" (Daily & Weekly Focus)
- At the start of each week: Define the **Top 3 High-Impact Outcomes** that would make the week a success.
- At the start of each day: Define the **1 Must-Win Task** and **2 Supporting Tasks**. Ignore everything else until the Must-Win task is completed.

## 2. Energy & Chronotype Matching

Manage energy, not just calendar minutes:
- **Peak Energy Block (Morning / Chronotype High)**: High-cognitive load tasks (writing, strategic thinking, hard synthesis, complex problem solving). Strictly zero meetings, zero email.
- **Trough Energy Block (Early Afternoon)**: Low-cognitive load tasks (administrative work, clearing inbox, scheduling, filing).
- **Recovery / Social Block (Late Afternoon)**: Collaboration, brainstorms, 1-on-1s, physical movement.

## 3. Time Blocking & Deep Work Rituals

- **The 90-Minute Focus Block**: Work for 90 minutes in complete isolation (airplane mode, notifications off), followed by a 15–20 minute real break away from screens.
- **Batch Processing**: Group similar tasks together (e.g., answer all emails at 11:30 AM and 4:30 PM rather than reacting continually throughout the day).
- **Context-Switching Minimization**: Guard against the "attention residue" of switching between multiple open tabs or projects.

## 4. The GTD Weekly Review Ritual
Perform this 30-minute review every Friday afternoon or Sunday evening:
1. **Clear the Decks**: Empty physical notes, desktop downloads, and inbox into a single trusted system.
2. **Review Open Loops**: Review active projects, pending deliverables, and calendar for the next 14 days.
3. **Calibrate Priorities**: Select the next week's Top 3 priorities and block time on the calendar for them.
4. **Shutdown Confirmation**: Give yourself psychological permission to detach completely over the weekend.

## 5. Procrastination Interventions
- **The 5-Minute Activation Rule**: If resisting a task, commit to working on it for just 5 minutes. The friction is in the start, not the execution.
- **Implementation Intentions**: Frame tasks as: *"When [Trigger/Time/Location] occurs, I will immediately do [Specific Action]."*
