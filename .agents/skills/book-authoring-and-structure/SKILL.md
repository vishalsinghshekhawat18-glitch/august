# Book Authoring & Manuscript Architecture

This skill guides authors through the complete lifecycle of creating a book—from the initial premise and outline to chapter drafting, narrative pacing, and revision.

## Phase 1: Core Concept & Positioning (The Book Canvas)
Before writing a single chapter, crystallize the foundation:
- **Title & Subtitle**: Compelling, memorable hook paired with a benefit-driven subtitle.
- **One-Sentence Core Proposition**: What is the single transformative idea this book delivers?
- **Target Reader & Need**: Who is this written for, what pain or curiosity are they experiencing, and who is this *not* for?
- **Transformation Arc**: The reader starts at State A (struggling, curious, uninformed) and finishes at State B (equipped, enlightened, empowered).
- **Comparative Analysis (Comp Titles)**: 3 similar books and how this book differs or goes further.

## Phase 2: Structural Architecture

### For Non-Fiction / Argument-Driven Books
Structure the book into 3–4 overarching thematic parts (e.g., *Part I: The Problem, Part II: The Framework, Part III: The Execution*).

#### Standard Chapter Blueprint (The 5-Part Model)
1. **The Narrative Hook (10–15% of chapter)**: A gripping story, historical anecdote, or counter-intuitive case study introducing the chapter's stakes.
2. **The Core Thesis (15–20%)**: Clearly define the concept, principle, or mechanism at play.
3. **The Evidence & Nuance (30–40%)**: Deep-dive supporting arguments, research, and addressing the common objections or failure modes.
4. **Actionable Application (20–25%)**: Practical frameworks, exercises, or step-by-step methodologies the reader can apply immediately.
5. **Chapter Debrief & Transition (5%)**: 3 key takeaways and a forward-looking hook to the next chapter.

### For Fiction / Narrative Works
- **Structure**: 3-Act Structure, Hero's Journey, or Snowflake Method.
- **Scene Planning**: Goal ➔ Conflict ➔ Disaster (Scene) followed by Reaction ➔ Dilemma ➔ Decision (Sequel).

## Phase 3: Manuscript Development & Cadence
- **Word Count Budgeting**:
  - Commercial Non-Fiction: 50,000 – 70,000 words (~10–12 chapters of 5,000 words each).
  - Fiction / Novels: 70,000 – 90,000 words.
  - Short Guides / Manifestos: 25,000 – 40,000 words.
- **Writing Cadence**: Separation of the drafting mind (flow, speed, uninhibited creation) from the editing mind (structure, style, pruning).

## Phase 4: Revision & Pruning
- **Structural Edit**: Do the chapters build logically? Are there redundant arguments?
- **Line Edit**: Voice consistency, rhythm, cadence, and trimming unnecessary filler.
- **Reader Engagement Check**: Does every chapter deliver on the promise made in its opening lines?
