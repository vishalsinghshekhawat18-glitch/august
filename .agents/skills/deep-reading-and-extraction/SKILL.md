# Deep Reading & Knowledge Extraction

This skill approaches books and dense texts not as passive summaries, but through Mortimer Adler's framework in *How to Read a Book*—extracting enduring mental models, debating propositions, and converting insights into practical wisdom.

## The 3 Levels of Analytical Reading

### Level 1: Inspectional Reading (Structural Anatomy)
- **Classification**: What kind of book is this (practical philosophy, historical analysis, behavioral psychology, theoretical treatise)?
- **Overarching Thesis**: In 1–2 sentences, what is the author trying to prove, solve, or explain?
- **Structural Blueprint**: How has the author partitioned the argument across major sections?

### Level 2: Analytical Reading (Interpreting & Questioning)
- **Coming to Terms with the Author**: Identify the author's unique vocabulary, keywords, and specific definitions (e.g., how Kahneman defines "System 1", or Taleb defines "Antifragility").
- **Core Propositions**: What are the author's most important factual assertions and foundational axioms?
- **The Argument Chain**: How does the author bridge their axioms to their final conclusions? Where are the logical leaps or unstated assumptions?
- **Critical Evaluation**: Is the author informed, uninformed, misinformed, or incomplete in their logic?

### Level 3: Syntopical Synthesis (Dialectical Insight)
- How does this work compare or disagree with opposing thinkers on the same subject?
- What eternal questions does this text answer, and what new dilemmas does it introduce?

## Knowledge Extraction Template

When processing a book or text, produce this comprehensive breakdown:

```markdown
# [Book Title] — Deep Knowledge Extraction
**Author**: [Author Name] | **Year**: [Year] | **Core Domain**: [Domain]

### 1. The Core Thesis (The One Big Idea)
[Single paragraph summarizing the central argument that unifies the entire book.]

### 2. Foundational Mental Models & Frameworks
- **[Model 1 Name]**: Description, underlying mechanism, and real-world application.
- **[Model 2 Name]**: Description, underlying mechanism, and real-world application.

### 3. Key Terminology & Precise Definitions
- **[Term]**: What the author specifically means by this word vs. conventional usage.

### 4. The 5 Most Illuminating Quotes / Passages
1. *"Quote"* — Context and philosophical significance.

### 5. Critical Critique & Blind Spots
- Where does the theory break down in practice?
- What context has changed since the author wrote this?

### 6. Actionable Takeaways & Behavioral Changes
- [Rule 1]: Daily habit or decision rule derived from the work.
- [Rule 2]: Warning sign or heuristic to watch for.
```
