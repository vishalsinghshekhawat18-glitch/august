Specific Legal & Administrative Core:

• Judiciary in the Constitution: Articles 124–147 (Supreme Court composition, jurisdiction, appointment of judges, removal, Court of Record, advisory jurisdiction, curative petitions), Articles 214–231 (High Courts), Writ Jurisdiction (Articles 32 & 226), Judicial Review, Basic Structure doctrine.

• Supreme Court Rules, 2013: Procedural regulations, powers of registrar, listing of cases, lodging petitions (SLPs, Appeals, Caveats), affidavits, court fees, paper books.

• Supreme Court Officers & Servants Rules, 1961: Hierarchy, conduct rules, disciplinary action, leave, attendance, office confidentiality.

• General English & Aptitude: Grammar, vocabulary, error correction, legal maxims and terms, syllogisms, series, analogies, basic percentages, ratio & averages.

Stage 2 & 3: Typing Test, Descriptive Test & Interview

• Typing Test on Computer: English typing test with minimum speed of 35 words per minute (w.p.m.) after 10 minutes test (Mistakes allowed up to 3%).

• Descriptive Written Test: Duration: 2 Hours. Consists of English Comprehension Passage, Précis Writing, and Essay Writing.

• Interview: Personal interview assessing court suitability, temperament, and communication skills.

4 RVUNL: Junior Engineer-I (Electrical) — Detailed Syllabus (Advt: 2026-27/02)
CBT: Nov 29 & 30, 2026 ▼
Computer Based Test (CBT) Structure 2 Hours • 140 Questions • 200 Marks
Part Scope Weightage Level
Part 'A' Electrical Engineering Degree Core 60% (120 Marks) Graduation / B.Tech Level
Part 'B' General Awareness, Reasoning, Maths, Hindi & English 40% (80 Marks) 10th / 12th Standard Level
Part 'A' — Electrical Engineering Degree Syllabus (60%):

• Electrical Circuits & Networks: Mesh and nodal analysis, Thevenin’s, Norton’s, Superposition, Maximum Power Transfer theorems, transient response of RL, RC, RLC circuits, sinusoidal steady-state analysis, resonance, three-phase balanced and unbalanced circuits.

• Electrical Machines: Single-phase and three-phase transformers (equivalent circuit, testing, parallel operation, autotransformers), DC machines (characteristics, starting, braking, speed control), Three-phase induction motors (torque-speed, slip, starting methods), Synchronous machines (voltage regulation, V-curves, parallel operation, hunting).

• Power Systems: Generation, transmission line parameters, performance of lines, sag calculation, underground cables, load flow studies, fault analysis (symmetrical & unsymmetrical), circuit breakers, protective relays, power system stability.

• Control Systems: Transfer functions, block diagrams, signal flow graphs, time response, Routh-Hurwitz, Bode plot, Nyquist criterion.

• Power Electronics & Drives: SCR, MOSFET, IGBT characteristics, Phase controlled rectifiers, inverters, choppers, AC voltage controllers, electric drive speed control.

• Measurements & Instrumentation: Bridges, PMMC, MI meters, wattmeters, energy meters, digital voltmeters, instrument transformers (CT/PT).

Part 'B' — Non-Technical & Rajasthan GK (40%):

• Rajasthan General Knowledge: History, Art, Culture, Architecture, Heritage, Geography, Natural Resources, and Socio-Economic development of Rajasthan.

• Everyday Science & Current Affairs: Basic scientific principles, current events of Rajasthan and national importance.

• Reasoning & Mental Ability: Analogies, Series, Coding-Decoding, Blood relations, Venn diagrams, Decision making.

• Mathematics: 10th standard arithmetic, algebra, mensuration, trigonometry basics.

• General Hindi & General English: Grammar, spelling errors, idioms, antonyms/synonyms, comprehension.
